Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products safe for young children?

Yes! Safety is our top priority. Our ingredients are thoroughly tested and sourced from certified suppliers to ensure they are safe for kids of all ages.

Do your candies contain artificial dyes or chemicals?

We minimize synthetic ingredients wherever possible by using natural colors derived from fruits and vegetables, such as beet juice and turmeric.

Are any of your products nut-free or allergen-friendly?

Many of our gummies and lollipops are made in nut-free facilities. Please check individual product packaging for specific allergen information.

How should I store these candies at home?

Keep your sweets in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to preserve their freshness, flavor, and texture.
